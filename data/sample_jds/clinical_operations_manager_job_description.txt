TITLE: Clinical Operations Manager
DEPARTMENT: Outpatient Specialty Services
EMPLOYMENT TYPE: Full-Time / Onsite
LOCATION: Denver, CO

ABOUT THE ROLE:
The Clinical Operations Manager oversees daily administrative and ambulatory care workflows across three outpatient specialty clinics. This role ensures clinical safety, regulatory accreditation, optimal staffing allocation, and an empathetic patient experience.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Direct front-office and clinical support personnel (medical assistants, receptionists, triage coordinators), overseeing scheduling, performance reviews, and clinical onboarding.
- Maintain continuous compliance with Joint Commission (TJC), CMS, OSHA, and HIPAA guidelines across all clinic operations.
- Supervise clinical supply chain logistics, pharmaceutical inventory audits, equipment calibration schedules, and sterile processing compliance.
- Track operational clinic metrics including average patient wait times, room turnover velocity, no-show rates, and provider schedule density.
- Lead root-cause analyses on incident reports and patient feedback to execute corrective action plans (CAPAs).

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- Bachelor’s degree in Healthcare Administration, Nursing, Business Administration, or related discipline (BSN/RN or MHA preferred).
- 4+ years of healthcare supervisory experience within an ambulatory clinic, surgical center, or hospital outpatient department.
- Demonstrated command of electronic health record (EHR) systems (e.g., Epic, Cerner) and practice management software.
- Active Basic Life Support (BLS) certification or eligibility to obtain within 90 days of hire.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- Lean Six Sigma (Green Belt) in healthcare operational improvement.
- Background managing multi-site clinical facilities and unionized healthcare workforces.

COMPENSATION & PERKS:
- Base Salary Range: $95,000 – $118,000 USD
- Full institutional benefits (health, prescription, life, and disability)
- 403(b) retirement plan with employer contribution
- 4 weeks Paid Time Off (PTO) plus 10 recognized holidays
- Relocation assistance package for candidates outside Colorado
