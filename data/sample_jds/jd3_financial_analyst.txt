Financial Analyst

About the role
Our corporate finance team needs a Financial Analyst to support planning, valuation and reporting.

Responsibilities
- Build financial models and forecasts.
- Prepare valuation analysis for investment decisions.
- Produce monthly financial reports for management.

Requirements
- At least 3 years of experience in financial analysis.
- Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting or Economics. MBA or CFA is a plus.
- Expertise in financial modeling and valuation.
- Advanced Excel skills.
- Experience with financial reporting.

Preferred
- Power BI.
- SQL.
- CFA certification.
