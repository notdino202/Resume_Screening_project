TITLE: Brand Editorial Director
DEPARTMENT: Brand & Communications
EMPLOYMENT TYPE: Full-Time / Remote
LOCATION: Remote (US-based)

ABOUT THE ROLE:
We are looking for a visionary Brand Editorial Director to lead narrative strategy, multimedia storytelling, and voice consistency across all customer-facing touchpoints. You will oversee a multidisciplinary team of staff writers, copywriters, researchers, and freelance creatives to turn complex ideas into compelling editorial content.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Build and execute the annual content and editorial roadmap across web publications, thought leadership essays, multimedia series, and whitepapers.
- Own and evolve the corporate style guide, tone guidelines, and lexicon to ensure distinctive brand positioning in the market.
- Manage the editorial calendar, review cycles, and fact-checking workflows for fast-paced multi-channel distribution.
- Collaborate with Product Marketing, Social, and Performance teams to ensure editorial initiatives drive measurable engagement and brand equity.
- Mentor and develop junior copywriters, editors, and external contributors through constructive critique and editorial workshops.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- 7+ years of editorial, journalism, or content direction experience at a high-growth brand, major publication, or creative agency.
- Portfolio demonstrating high-level conceptual writing, long-form journalism/essays, and creative campaign direction.
- Proven leadership skills with experience managing editors, copywriters, and freelance networks.
- Deep understanding of organic distribution channels, SEO best practices, and qualitative audience analytics.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- Experience producing scriptwriting or creative direction for narrative podcasts or video mini-docs.
- Familiarity with headless CMS platforms (Contentful, Sanity) and collaborative editorial tools (Notion, Figma).

COMPENSATION & PERKS:
- Base Salary Range: $140,000 – $170,000 USD
- Equity incentive package
- Unlimited Flexible Time Off (FTO) policy with mandatory minimums
- Home office stipend ($1,500 initial setup) and monthly wellness allowance
