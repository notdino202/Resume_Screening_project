Data Scientist

About the role
We are hiring a Data Scientist to build predictive models that improve our customer retention and pricing decisions.

Responsibilities
- Build, validate and deploy machine learning models.
- Analyze large datasets and communicate findings to business teams.
- Work with engineers to put models into production.

Requirements
- 3+ years of experience in data science or analytics.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics or a related field.
- Strong proficiency in Python and SQL.
- Hands-on experience with machine learning.

Preferred
- Experience with Apache Spark.
- Familiarity with AWS.
