Digital Marketing Manager

About the role
We are looking for a Digital Marketing Manager to own our online growth strategy and lead a small team.

Responsibilities
- Plan and run multi-channel digital campaigns.
- Grow organic traffic and lead generation.
- Report on performance and campaign ROI.

Requirements
- Minimum 4 years of experience in digital marketing.
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business or a related field.
- Strong knowledge of SEO and Google Analytics.
- Proven content strategy and campaign management experience.

Nice to have
- Experience with HubSpot.
- Knowledge of A/B testing.
- Basic SQL.
